## Content work (portfolio, CV, site copy)

Content standards live in `.claude/docs/` and are not loaded
automatically.

- Case study pages and their `facts.md`: use the
  `portfolio-case-study-writing` skill.
- Other writing (CV, About page, project cards, LinkedIn): first read
  `.claude/docs/writing_guidelines.md` and `.claude/docs/quality_criteria.md`;
  for CV work also `.claude/docs/decisions_cv.md`.
- Job search questions: `.claude/docs/decisions_job_search.md` and
  `.claude/docs/market_context.md`.

Each case study folder holds the page, `notes.md` (the designer's raw
material, any language) and `facts.md` (checked facts with status).
Text is written from `facts.md` only. Never invent facts; mark gaps as
`[GAP: question]`.

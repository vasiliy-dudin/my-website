# Job Search Decisions

Verdicts and basis labels are defined in `decisions_rules.md`.

## Channels

| Recommendation | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Referrals and warm intros before mass cold applying | Do | [vendor] platform datasets converge on referrals converting several times better; direction undisputed |
| Recruiter-only "open to work" signal | Do | [none] near-zero cost, no downside identified |
| Public "open to work" banner | Ask first | [reported] genuinely mixed: a meaningful minority of managers read it negatively at senior level |
| Direct outreach to the hiring manager with a specific reason | Do, flag once | [study] adjacent-field (engineering) ranking; plausible transfer; low cost |
| Building the strategy on agency recruiter inbound | Don't | [reported] lowest-ranked channel in adjacent-field data; passive by design |
| Optimising the LinkedIn profile for recruiter search before applying at volume | Do | [mechanic] sourcing works through search; cheap |

## Volume, timing, interpretation

| Recommendation | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Targeted applications to a prioritised list, light tailoring each | Do | [inference] from base rates and retrieval mechanics |
| Spray-and-pray volume at senior level | Don't | [vendor] cold conversion is low single digits; volume destroys tailoring and morale |
| Apply early after posting | Do | [inference] plausible mechanism; zero cost |
| Believing specific timing multipliers ("8× within 96 hours", best day of week) | Don't | [vendor] single 2017 self-report by a timing-software company; untraceable beyond it |
| Early applications to lower-priority companies as pipeline and interview practice | Do | [inference] walkthrough quality improves with reps; low cost |
| Track own funnel (applications → screens → onsites) and treat it as the primary dataset | Do | [inference] published base rates are unreliable — see `market_context.md` known gaps |
| Interpreting any single rejection or silence as signal | Don't | [mechanic] ghost jobs, bulk disposition and knockouts produce noise; judge patterns over 20+ applications |
| If cold conversion stays under ~1% over 20+ applications, shift effort to referrals and direct outreach | Do, flag once | [inference] planning heuristic, not a measured threshold |

## Interviews and offer

| Recommendation | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Rehearse a spoken 15–30 minute walkthrough of 1–2 cases, out loud, with a live listener where possible | Do | [mechanic] the loop includes it; [reported] strongest-consensus prep |
| Practise live design challenges and app critiques | Do | [reported] increasingly the first round in 2026 |
| Thank-you note after interviews | Do | [none] etiquette; costless; no effect claimed |
| Expecting follow-ups to change outcomes | Don't | [none] no evidence of effect |
| Counter-offer 10–20% above initial (5–7% if already mid-band) | Do, flag once | [vendor] US-weighted surveys; no UK/EU design-specific data |
| If base is fixed: negotiate title, scope, or a 6-month review | Do, flag once | [reported] UK recruiter accounts |
| Fearing rescission for a polite counter | Don't | [none] described as very rare; no credible contrary data |
